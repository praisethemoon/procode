Knowledge icon: twenty options to replace the spiral lens

Twenty candidates for the Knowledge view's activity bar icon, drawn in the set's language: one 2px round stroke on a 24×24 canvas, outline only. Each is shown large, at 48, 24 and 16px, dimmed as an inactive view, and in the bar beside lap, the Board and eggzibit. Click one to put it in the lineup.

In the activity bar

Recommended: 1. Open book, or 3. Stack of books.

The open book says knowledge at once and is the clearest at 16px; its only drawback is resembling the codicon the view started with. The stack of books looks like nothing else in the bar and suggests collections, which the sidebar now opens on (T-230). Among the rest, 9. Linked notes is the one that says what kb uniquely does (links between documents), at the cost of reading as “share”.

What they avoid.

None uses lap's spiral (the reason the lens looked like lap's twin) or an egg. Each was checked for how it reads beside the other three at 24px and on its own at 16px; the risk line on every card says what it might be mistaken for.
