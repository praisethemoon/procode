Board icon: twenty options to replace the square spiral

Twenty candidates for the Board view's activity bar icon (coboard), drawn in the set's language: one 2px round stroke on a 24×24 canvas, outline only. Each is shown large, at 48, 24 and 16px, dimmed as an inactive view, and in the bar in the Board's place, between lap and eggzibit, with Knowledge's new Linked notes below. Click one to put it in the lineup.

In the activity bar

Recommended: 1. Pushpin, or 16. Honeycomb.

The pushpin is one clean shape that says “on the board”, reads at 16px, and looks like nothing else in the bar. The honeycomb is the most distinctive of the twenty and fits the “co” in coboard: many working together, cell by cell; its risk is reading as a hive or a molecule. 2. Easel is the plain-spoken alternative. Two did not come out well and say so on their cards: 5. Stamp and 18. Fanned cards.

What they avoid.

A second round: none repeats the first twenty (lanes, tickets, checklists, notes, flags, trees and the rest, which the user turned down), none uses lap's spiral or an egg, and none uses nodes and links, now Knowledge's. Each was checked beside the other three at 24px and alone at 16px; the risk line on every card says what it might be mistaken for.
