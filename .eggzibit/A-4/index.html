Board icon: twenty options to replace the square spiral

Twenty candidates for the Board view's activity bar icon (coboard), drawn in the set's language: one 2px round stroke on a 24×24 canvas, outline only. Each is shown large, at 48, 24 and 16px, dimmed as an inactive view, and in the bar in the Board's place, between lap and eggzibit, with Knowledge's new Linked notes below. Click one to put it in the lineup.

In the activity bar

Recommended: 1. Lanes, or 3. Ticket.

Lanes says “board” at once, is the view's own layout, and its three upright bars sit well between lap's spiral and the egg, apart from Knowledge's circles and lines; its one risk is looking like Trello's mark. The ticket is the most distinctive of the twenty and names what the board is made of. 8. Pinned note is the calm alternative if both feel too literal.

What they avoid.

None uses lap's spiral, which the square spiral borrows, or an egg. Linked notes is now Knowledge's, so no board option uses nodes and links. Each was checked beside the other three at 24px and alone at 16px; the risk line on every card says what it might be mistaken for.
