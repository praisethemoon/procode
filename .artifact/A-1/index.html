1. Lineups

Recommended: one spiral besides lap's, not three.

Spiral lens gives Knowledge lap's look and a clear meaning (search). Lanes and Folded page say what they open without help. The all-spiral set is the most unified, but four spirals in one bar make the views hard to tell apart at a glance.

2. What is taken from lap

  • One stroke weight: 2px on a 24px canvas, round ends and round joins (Strata keeps sharp joins where layers meet).
  • The canvas filled: drawings span about 18px, leaving about 2px of margin, as lap's does.
  • Outline only: no filled shapes. VS Code draws activity bar icons as a single-colour mask, so only the outline counts.
  • The spiral itself in three options (Square spiral, Page from the spiral, Spiral lens), each with the same outward passes as lap's.
